## Consequences for the protocol

- **G1, mechanism and fix-reform dates:** confirmed. The validation window 2015–2023 is entirely post-publication (Melvin & Prins published 2015, online in late 2014) and post-reform (15 Feb 2015).
- **G1, post-2015 month-end evidence:** none found in primary sources. Krohn et al. explicitly separate their (persisting) daily fix reversals from the month-end hedging channel. The prior for G1 in validation therefore rests on unverified decay.
- **FRED terms (needs your decision, item D-FRED in the Study 5 protocol):**
  - The project has stored FRED series locally since 2026-10-22: DEXJPUS, DTB3 and others, plus DEXUSUK today. All are tagged public domain and kept in git-ignored `data/`, never redistributed.
  - FRED's Full Terms prohibit storing or archiving "FRED Content", while the public-domain tag permits use without permission. Which clause governs public-domain series isn't stated.
  - Options:
    - (a) accept, as local private research use of public-domain series;
    - (b) take H.10 data from the Board's own XML files (https://www.federalreserve.gov/releases/h10/data/FRB_h10_xml.zip), which the Board publishes directly;
    - (c) ask FRED.
  - **Recommendation: (b) for H.10 series** before Study 5 runs. It is the primary publisher's file, it removes the ambiguity, and it is a fresh download (needs your approval).
