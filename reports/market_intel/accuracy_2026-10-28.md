## What this does and does not show

- It measures agreement between Yahoo's indicative price and two official references at documented times, over the few days Yahoo keeps 1-minute history (about 7).
- It cannot verify executable prices, bid/ask spreads (Yahoo's FX bid/ask fields are rejected) or gold: GLD and COMEX futures have no independent licensed reference here, and XAU/USD spot isn't available.
- ECB times are approximate ('normally around 14:10 CET'); H.10 rates are buying rates, not mid-market rates; the rounding column bounds the error from published decimals only.
